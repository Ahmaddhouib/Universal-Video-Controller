About Universal Video Controller

CONTROL ANY VIDEO ON THE WEB JUST BY HOVERING OVER IT.

Universal Video Controller is completely free and unlimited

Universal Video Controller started as a personal passion project built out of a simple desire to make watching videos online a better experience. As a solo developer, I created this extension to solve the everyday frustrations I was running into myself—and I’m excited to share it with anyone else looking for smoother, more intuitive control over their web videos.

Auto-Scroll

Automatically scroll or advance to the next video when a video ends on short-form platforms.

Loop State & Auto-Scroll Rules
  • Auto-Disables Looping: Auto-Scroll automatically disables video looping on activated URLs so videos reach the end and scroll.
  • User Loop Pause: When loop is enabled by the user in the video (via controller loop button or L), Auto-Scroll will be deactivated until loop is turned off.
